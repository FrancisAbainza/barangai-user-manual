Part 1

Getting started

Do these three things once. After that, every service in the portal is open to you.

Create an account and sign in

  1. Open the Barangay Maduya website. You can read the news, transparency projects and About Us pages without an account.
  2. Click Sign Up at the top of the page (on a phone, open the menu first).
  3. Fill in the sign-up form and follow the on-screen steps to verify your email address.
  4. Next time, click Sign In. You will go straight to the portal's Home page.
New accounts are resident accounts. Barangay staff are given admin access by a Super Admin after they sign up. See Admin roles.

Find your way around

  • On a computer: the menu runs down the left side of the screen. Click the small panel button at its top edge to shrink it to icons.
  • On a phone: tap the menu button at the top-left to open the menu. It closes when you pick a page.
  • Your account: click your profile picture at the bottom of the menu. Choose My Profile to see your details and your past requests, or Manage account to change your name, photo, email or password. You can also sign out here.
  • Light or dark screen: click the sun or moon button beside your name. The portal remembers your choice.

Fill in your Resident Credentials

Your Resident Credentials prove that you live in Barangay Maduya. You need them before you can request documents, file a complaint or reserve the court. You only do this once.

  1. Click your profile picture, then My Profile. You can also click Complete Resident Credentials on the Home page.
  2. Enter your full name, birthdate, sex, civil status, contact number and complete address (house or unit number, street and purok).
  3. Choose your valid ID type, such as the Philippine National ID, Passport, Driver's License, UMID, Voter's ID, PhilHealth, SSS or Postal ID.
  4. Upload a clear photo of the front and the back of that ID.
  5. Save. The services that were locked are now open.
Tip: take the ID photo in good light, flat on a table, with all four corners showing. Staff may reject a request if they cannot read your ID.
Part 2

For residents

Each section below matches one item in the portal's menu.

Home

Menu: Home

Home is your starting point. It shows:

  • Quick Actions: shortcuts to request a document, file a complaint, reserve the court and more.
  • My Activity: your latest requests, complaints and reservations with their current status.
  • Latest News: the newest barangay announcements.

Document Request

Menu: Document Request · Needs Resident Credentials

Request barangay certificates online, then pick them up at the Barangay Hall or receive a soft copy online.

DocumentUse it forWhat you provide
Barangay ClearanceEmployment, bank or loan requirements, business, legal transactionsPurpose and a GCash payment receipt. The fee depends on the purpose and is shown in the form.
Certificate of ResidencyProof that you live in the barangay (work, school, travel, government transactions)Purpose
Certificate of No ObjectionBusiness permits, construction or renovation, tricycle operation, events, use of public spacePurpose
Certificate of IndigencyMedical, financial, burial, scholarship or subsidy assistancePurpose, a short description of your situation, supporting documents
Solo Parent CertificationSolo Parent ID and benefitsPurpose, a short description of your situation, supporting documents
Medical / Lab AssistanceHospital, laboratory, medicine or surgery assistancePurpose, a short description of your situation, supporting documents

How to request

  1. Open Document Request and click the document you need.
  2. Choose your purpose. If it is not listed, pick Other and type it in.
  3. Barangay Clearance only: send the amount shown to the barangay's GCash number (also shown in the form), take a screenshot of the receipt, and upload it.
  4. Choose how to receive it: Pickup at Barangay Hall or Online Soft Copy.
  5. Click Submit Request. It appears under My Requests.

What happens next

Pending→Processing→Ready for PickuporRejected

You get a notification when your request is ready, with a message from staff and any attached files. If it is rejected, the notification explains why so you can fix it and submit again.

Complaint

Menu: Complaint · Needs Resident Credentials

Report a community concern such as noise, garbage, broken roads, flooding, traffic, water or electricity problems.

This is not for emergencies. For fires, crimes in progress or medical emergencies, call the emergency hotline or go to the Barangay Hall right away.
  1. Open Complaint and click Report a Complaint.
  2. Write a short Subject, then describe what happened in as much detail as you can.
  3. Mark the Location of Incident on the map. You can use your current location.
  4. Add photos or videos as Evidence if you have them.
  5. Submit. The portal sorts your complaint into a category and priority so the right staff see it first.
Pending→In Progress→ResolvedorDismissed

When your complaint is resolved or dismissed, you get a notification with a message from staff.

Court Reservation

Menu: Court Reservation · Needs Resident Credentials

Book the barangay court for games, practice or events.

  1. Click Availability to see which times are already booked.
  2. Click the button to reserve, then pick the Date and type the Purpose (for example, "Basketball practice").
  3. Pick a Start Time and how many hours you need. You can start at any minute, but you book in whole hours and must finish by midnight.
  4. Check the Total Amount Due. Daytime (6:00 AM to 6:00 PM) and nighttime (6:00 PM to 6:00 AM) have different hourly rates, and the form shows the charge for each hour.
  5. Send the total to the barangay's GCash number, upload a screenshot of the receipt, and submit.
Pending→ApprovedorRejected
Overlap warning: if your time overlaps another booking, the form tells you before you submit. Pick a different time.

Community Hub

Menu: Community Hub

A directory of verified local businesses in Barangay Maduya.

Find a business

  • Scroll the list or switch to the map to see businesses near you.
  • Use the filter to narrow by category (Food & Beverage, Retail, Services and more) or to show only your own businesses.
  • Open a business to see its contact number, social media page, opening days and hours, and photos.

List your business

  1. Click the button to submit a business.
  2. Enter the name, description, category, contact number and (optional) social media link.
  3. Tick the days you are open and set opening and closing times.
  4. Upload photos, a copy of your business permit, and pin the location on the map.
  5. Submit. Staff check it before it appears to everyone.
Pending→Processing→VerifiedorRejected

News & Announcements

Menu: News & Announcements

Official posts from the barangay, labelled Announcement, Event or Emergency. On each post you can:

  • Like or Dislike it.
  • Comment and reply to other residents.
  • Share a link to it.

Transparency

Menu: Transparency

See what the barangay is building and spending on: infrastructure, health, education, livelihood and more. Each project shows its description, budget, photos, attached files and location on the map. You can react to and comment on projects.

About Us

Menu: About Us

The barangay's mission and vision, and the current Barangay and Sangguniang Kabataan (SK) officials.

Notifications

Menu: Notifications

Every status change on your document requests, complaints, court reservations and business listings shows up here. A number beside Notifications in the menu tells you how many you have not read yet. Click a notification to open the related item.

AI Assistant

The round chat button in the bottom corner of every portal page

Ask questions about barangay services in plain words. For example:

  • "What do I need for a Certificate of Indigency?"
  • "How much is the court at night?"
  • "I want to report uncollected garbage on our street."

When your question matches a form or page, the assistant shows a button under its answer. Click it to go straight to that form.

To speak instead of typing, click the microphone, talk, then click it again. Your browser will ask for microphone permission the first time.

Remember: the assistant gives general guidance. Official decisions on your requests come from barangay staff.
Part 3

For barangay staff

Admins see the same menu as residents plus User Management and Barangay Settings. On each service page, admins see every resident's submissions instead of only their own.

Admin roles

RoleCan do
Residentdefault for every new accountUse all resident services in Part 2.
AdminReview and update all submissions, post news and projects, ban or unban users, change barangay settings, manage the AI knowledge base.
Super AdminEverything an Admin can do, plus change other users' roles and delete user accounts.

Your role is shown under your name at the bottom of the menu.

Admin Home

Menu: Home

  • Needs Attention: the newest submissions waiting for action. Click one to open it. When this says All caught up, nothing is waiting.
  • Pending summary: counts of pending requests in each service.
  • Shortcuts: quick links to management pages.

Reviewing submissions

Document Request, Complaint, Court Reservation and Community Hub pages

All four services work the same way.

  1. Open the page. Use the filters to narrow by status, category, priority or date.
  2. Find the row and click its actions button (the three dots).
  3. Choose Submission Info to see everything the resident sent, including their Resident Credentials, ID photos, payment receipt and attachments.
  4. Choose Set status and pick the new status. The resident is notified automatically.

Some statuses ask you for a message, which the resident sees in their notification:

Setting this statusAsks you for
Document: Ready for PickupA message (for example, where and when to claim it) and optional attachments, such as the soft copy
Document: RejectedThe reason, so the resident can correct it
Complaint: ResolvedA message describing what was done, with optional photos
Complaint: DismissedThe reason for dismissing it
Court: RejectedThe reason
Business: RejectedThe reason (for example, an unreadable permit)

Extra tools

  • Complaints: inside Submission Info, click Get AI-generated insights for a suggested solution, budget estimate, manpower, timeframe, public safety risk and prevention advice. Use it as a starting point only.
  • Complaints and Community Hub: switch to the map view to see where submissions are.
  • Court Reservation: admins can book the court for barangay activities. The GCash receipt is optional for admin bookings.
  • Community Hub: admins can edit a listing. Businesses added by an admin are verified right away.
  • Delete in the actions menu removes the record permanently.

Posting news and transparency projects

Menu: News & Announcements or Transparency

  1. Click the create button at the top of the page.
  2. News: enter a title, pick Announcement, Event or Emergency, write the content and add photos or videos.
    Project: enter a title, category, description, budget, location on the map, photos and attached files (for example, a budget report PDF).
  3. Publish. To change or remove a post later, use the edit or delete option on the post itself.

Barangay Settings

Menu: Barangay Settings

  • Document Pricing: the GCash number residents pay to, and the Barangay Clearance fee for each purpose.
  • Court Reservation Fees: the daytime and nighttime hourly rates.
  • Mission & Vision: the text shown on About Us.
  • Barangay Officials and Sangguniang Kabataan: add, edit or remove officials, with their position and photo.

Changes take effect right away for everyone.

AI Knowledge Base

AI Assistant chat button → Knowledge Base tab

The AI Assistant answers from one PDF document that you upload, such as a citizen's charter listing requirements, fees and office hours.

  1. Open the AI Assistant and switch to the Knowledge Base tab.
  2. Drop in or choose a PDF file.
  3. Wait until it finishes processing. This can take up to a minute.
Uploading a new PDF replaces the old one. Put all the information you want the assistant to know into a single file.

Common questions

I can't request a document, file a complaint or reserve the court.

Fill in your Resident Credentials first. These services stay locked until you do.

How do I pay?

Payments are by GCash only. Send the amount shown in the form to the barangay's GCash number, take a screenshot of the receipt, and upload it with your request. Only Barangay Clearance and court reservations need payment.

My request was rejected. What now?

Open the notification to read the reason, fix the problem (for example, upload a clearer ID or receipt), then submit a new request.

The microphone button doesn't work.

Your browser blocked the microphone. Click the lock icon in the address bar, allow the microphone for this site, and reload the page.

I can't sign in.

Use the forgot-password option on the sign-in screen to reset your password. If it says your account is banned, visit the Barangay Hall.

How do I change my name, email or password?

Click your profile picture at the bottom of the menu and choose Manage account. To change your address or ID, edit your Resident Credentials on My Profile.
